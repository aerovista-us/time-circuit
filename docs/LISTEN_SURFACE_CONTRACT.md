# Time Circuit Listen Surface Contract

The **Listen** view is a Time Circuit-native experience. Northline is not a design source for this surface.

The Listen view preserves:

- the large cover-art + visualizer composition;
- SVG triangle, core, bolt, waveform, frequency bars, and speed line;
- WebAudio analyzer-driven waveform and frequency response;
- the TIME CIRCUITS `DEST` / `FLUX` readout;
- Prev / Play / Next / Restart controls;
- seek, duration, and volume controls;
- the TC-01 through TC-04 numbered playlist with missing masters shown as pending;
- Media Session metadata and automatic advance across actual playable numbered masters.

Northline-inspired presentation is limited to the **Store** view. Archive remains Time Circuit-native as well.
